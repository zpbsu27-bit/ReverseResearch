# Identity and AD

Canonical graph nodes may represent host, identity, group, service, application, trust, session, certificate template, cloud principal, role, vulnerability, and data asset.

Use typed edges such as member-of, admin-of, trusts, can-assume, authenticates-to, reachable-from, vulnerable-to, and can-access. Keep credentials outside the graph as opaque references. Imported BloodHound/AD/IAM relationships remain observations until currentness and evidence are checked.
